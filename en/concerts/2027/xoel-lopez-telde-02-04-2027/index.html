About Xoel López

Xoel López is a leading voice in the Ibero-American independent music scene. Across more than twenty years, his records have reflected his journeys, discoveries and musical influences. From Elephant Band and Deluxe to his solo career, he has continued to explore new creative paths.

Oniria Popular is his 17th studio album, an intimate yet collective exploration of identity and human emotions, bringing contemporary pop into dialogue with traditional and folk music.
