About the documentary

Four decades after first stepping onto a stage, Fermín Muguruza embarks on his last large-scale tour: a collective journey celebrating 40 years of music, commitment and cultural resistance. The documentary follows the large-scale concerts, miles on the road and encounters along the way, from Kortatu and Negu Gorriak to his work today.

Moving between the stage and the more intimate moments of the journey, the film captures the close of a tour on this scale. Music, images and memory trace the path of an artist whose songs have accompanied generations.
