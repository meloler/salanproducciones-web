About Acantha Lang

Born in New Orleans and based in London, Acantha Lang brings soul, funk, blues and R&B together in music shaped by her roots and her voice.

Her debut album, Beautiful Dreams, was released in 2023. In 2026 she received the Female Vocalist of the Year award at the Boisdale Music Awards.

Why see her live

An opportunity to hear her voice and songs in a concert connecting New Orleans soul with funk, blues and R&B.
