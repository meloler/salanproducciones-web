About Mala Gestión

Mala Gestión are a band from Valencia presenting their second studio album, Hacemos lo que podemos, as part of their national tour. The record was made between 2025 and 2026 at Millenia Estudios in Valencia, produced by Diego Escriche.

The songs combine punk with electronic and metal influences, while also making room for softer passages. The album explores generational disillusionment and precarity through an energetic, contrasting sound.

A live night of punk and guitars at Sala Alboroto, featuring songs from Hacemos lo que podemos and other tracks from the band’s catalogue.
