About the trio

The Andrew Collins Trio is a Toronto-based Canadian roots music group led by mandolin virtuoso Andrew Collins. Their music brings together bluegrass, folk, Celtic music, jazz and Americana, with intricate arrangements, improvisation and onstage chemistry. The group received the Canadian Folk Music Award for Instrumental Group of the Year in 2014 and 2016.
