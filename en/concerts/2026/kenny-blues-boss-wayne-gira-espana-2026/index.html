About Kenny “Blues Boss” Wayne

Born in Spokane, Kenny has performed blues and boogie-woogie for over 50 years. He won the Juno for Best Blues Album in 2006, joined the Boogie-Woogie Piano Hall of Fame in 2017 and won the Pinetop Perkins Piano Player of the Year award at the 2024 Blues Music Awards.

Blues and boogie-woogie concerts in Spain 2026

Salán Producciones brings Kenny "Blues Boss" Wayne Band on his Spanish tour. Led by Juan Salán, a leading music promoter with more than 38 years of history in the Canary Islands and Spain, this tour visits 10 cities in September 2026: Donosti, Bilbao, Ponferrada, Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña, Valladolid, Zaragoza, Castellón, Valencia and Barcelona.

If you are looking for blues concerts in Spain, boogie-woogie in Valladolid, live music in Bilbao or piano blues in Barcelona, this is your chance. Kenny Wayne is one of the last great active masters of classic blues piano, with a live energy that leaves audiences speechless.

Follow us at salanproducciones.com to stay up to date with upcoming concerts in Spain and the Canary Islands.
