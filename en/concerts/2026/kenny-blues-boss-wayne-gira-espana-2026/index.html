About Kenny "Blues Boss" Wayne

Kenny Wayne was born in New Westminster, British Columbia, Canada, and was captivated by the boogie-woogie and blues he heard at home from an early age. With more than four decades on stage, he has become one of the world's most respected blues pianists. In 2017 he was inducted into the Boogie-Woogie Hall of Fame, and in 2024 he received the Best Blues Pianist award. His latest album won the Blues Blast Music Award 2025 and the Juno Award for Blues Album, Canada's Grammy equivalent.

With his full band, Kenny delivers a live show that blends power, humor and soul in equal measure. His piano moves between wild boogie and deep blues, pulling the audience in from the first chord. The Spain Tour 2026 is a unique chance to see him in intimate venues before the big stages claim him for good.

Blues and boogie-woogie concerts in Spain 2026

Salán Producciones brings Kenny "Blues Boss" Wayne Band on his first major Spanish tour. Led by Juan Salán, a leading music promoter with more than 38 years of history in the Canary Islands and Spain, this tour visits 10 cities in September 2026: Donosti, Bilbao, Ponferrada, Santiago de Compostela, A Coruña, Valladolid, Zaragoza, Castellón, Valencia and Barcelona.

If you are looking for blues concerts in Spain, boogie-woogie in Valladolid, live music in Bilbao or piano blues in Barcelona, this is your chance. Kenny Wayne is one of the last great active masters of classic blues piano, with a live energy that leaves audiences speechless.
